# 🍦 Ice Cream Edition

Hundreds of quick games for study breaks, all in **one file**. No install, no account, no setup.

## Download

1. Go to **[Releases](../../releases/latest)** (on the right side of this page).
2. Under **Assets**, click **`ice-cream-edition-local.html`** to download it.
3. Double-click the file. It opens in your browser and you're in.

You need an internet connection to play, because each game loads from the site that makes it.
If your browser asks whether to keep the file, choose **Keep**: it's a normal web page.

## What's inside

- **890+ games** in sections like Racing, Sports, Puzzle and Arcade, with search, favorites and recently played.
- **For You**: pick 5 games you like when you first open it, and the home page fills with picks for you.
  It's worked out on your computer from what you like, and nothing is sent anywhere.
- **Themes**: Overhauled, Legacy, Low (for slower Chromebooks), Basic, Minimal, plus layouts inspired by
  Steam, Roblox, YouTube, Spotify and The Pizza Edition. Make your own in Settings > Customize and share it with a code.
- **Shorts**: a grid of short videos that open on YouTube.
- **Notes** (or **Chat**, when it's open): the Chat tab asks first. Say no and it becomes private notes on your computer.
  Chat blocks swearing, slurs and links.
- **Break timer**, keyboard shortcuts, and lots of settings.

Your settings, favorites and notes are saved in your browser, on your computer.

## Updating

Download the newest file from [Releases](../../releases/latest) and use it instead of the old one.
Your settings carry over as long as you keep it in the same folder and open it in the same browser.
